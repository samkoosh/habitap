HabiTap

Privacy policy · Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version

HabiTap has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or location. Your scores and streak stay in your own browser.

What's stored on your device

HabiTap uses your browser's local storage to remember today's progress, your past daily results and your streak. This data never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. If you add HabiTap to your home screen, your browser also keeps a cached copy of the game so it opens quickly.

Services the game loads from

  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons for species photos and photo credits.
  • jsDelivr and unpkg for map outlines, and Google Fonts for the typeface.
  • The HabiTap puzzle server, which sends each day's species and checks your guesses. It receives the pin position you lock in, not who you are.

Like any website, these services see your IP address and browser details when your browser requests files from them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which features people use, such as how many people finish the daily or share a score. We don't use this to identify you, and we don't sell data.

Ads

HabiTap doesn't show ads right now. If that changes, this page will say which ad network is used and how to control ad personalization.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
