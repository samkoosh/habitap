What is Habitap?

Habitap is a free daily geography game about the world's most local wildlife. Every species in it lives in one small corner of the world, like a single island, lake, cave or mountain. Each day you get five of them, the same five for everyone, and you tap where you think each one lives on a 3D globe.

How to play

  1. Look at the photo and the name. Place names inside species names stay blurred until you guess.
  2. Spin and zoom the globe, then tap to drop your pin where you think the species lives.
  3. Tap Lock it in. You score up to 100 points, depending on how close you land, and learn why it lives only there.
  4. Stuck? A hint shows the region, for 20 points off.
  5. After five species, share your score and come back tomorrow to keep your streak going.

FAQ

How is my score calculated?

Each species is worth up to 100 points. A pin within 10 km scores the full 100, and the score drops gently the farther away you land. Using the region hint costs 20 points. The best possible daily score is 500.

When does the new puzzle come out?

A new set of five species unlocks every day at midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same five species for the same date.

Is Habitap free? Do I need an account?

Habitap is completely free and needs no account or sign-up. Your streak and progress are saved in your browser on your device.

Is Habitap like Worldle or GeoGuessr?

It's in the same spirit: a quick daily geography challenge you can share with friends. Instead of guessing a country or a street view, you guess where a rare plant or animal lives, and you learn why it lives only there.

Which species are in Habitap?

Every species is a strict endemic, meaning its whole wild range sits within roughly 150 km of one point, such as a single island, lake, cave or mountain. Examples include animals, plants, fish, birds and amphibians from every continent.

Can I play past puzzles?

Yes. After you finish the daily, the results screen lets you replay earlier dailies or play a practice round. Past puzzles don't affect your streak.

How do I share my score?

When you finish, tap Share result. On a phone it opens your share sheet, and on a computer it copies a short emoji summary of your five guesses with a link to the game.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Habitap works in any modern phone browser. You can also add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
